# Q1 2025 Results: Zone-by-Zone Summary

*2025-05-07 — Earnings Commentary*

Zone-by-zone results for Q1 2025:

- **North America**: revenue $3,364M (organic growth -4.7%), volume 19,842K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,087M (margin 32.3%).
- **Middle Americas**: revenue $3,784M (organic growth +3.6%), volume 35,081K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,858M (margin 49.1%).
- **South America**: revenue $2,978M (organic growth +8.5%), volume 40,891K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,007M (margin 33.8%).
- **EMEA**: revenue $1,965M (organic growth +4.8%), volume 20,752K hL, normalized EBITDA $624M (margin 31.8%).
- **Asia Pacific**: revenue $1,450M (organic growth -7.7%), volume 19,648K hL, normalized EBITDA $523M (margin 36.1%).

Middle Americas and South America led organic growth, with Brazil-driven strength in South America; North America and Asia Pacific both posted organic revenue declines.
