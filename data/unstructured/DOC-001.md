# Q1 2024 Results: Zone-by-Zone Summary

*2024-05-07 — Earnings Commentary*

Zone-by-zone results for Q1 2024:

- **North America**: revenue $3,593M (organic growth -8.8%), volume 21,353K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,126M (margin 31.3%).
- **Middle Americas**: revenue $4,051M (organic growth +8.0%), volume 35,690K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,886M (margin 46.6%).
- **South America**: revenue $3,233M (organic growth +5.1%), volume 40,347K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,084M (margin 33.5%).
- **EMEA**: revenue $1,927M (organic growth +16.3%), volume 21,030K hL, normalized EBITDA $569M (margin 29.5%).
- **Asia Pacific**: revenue $1,634M (organic growth -0.5%), volume 21,045K hL, normalized EBITDA $616M (margin 37.7%).

North America organic revenue declined as U.S. industry volumes remained soft, while EMEA posted the fastest organic growth of any zone, helped by premiumization in Europe and recovery in Nigeria and South Africa.
