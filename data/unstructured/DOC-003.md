# Q3 2024 Results: Zone-by-Zone Summary

*2024-10-30 — Earnings Commentary*

Zone-by-zone results for Q3 2024:

- **North America**: revenue $3,867M (organic growth +1.5%), volume 22,764K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,358M (margin 35.1%).
- **Middle Americas**: revenue $4,103M (organic growth +1.9%), volume 37,107K hL, normalized EBITDA $2,068M (margin 50.4%).
- **South America**: revenue $2,932M (organic growth +5.6%), volume 39,502K hL, normalized EBITDA $908M (margin 31.0%).
- **EMEA**: revenue $2,351M (organic growth +8.2%), volume 24,039K hL, normalized EBITDA $780M (margin 33.2%).
- **Asia Pacific**: revenue $1,691M (organic growth -9.5%), volume 24,514K hL, normalized EBITDA $503M (margin 29.7%).

North America returned to modest organic growth, while Asia Pacific's decline deepened further on continued China weakness -- a pattern that persisted through the rest of 2024.
