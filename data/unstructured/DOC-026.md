# Stella Artois Draught Expansion and On-Premise Recovery in the UK

*2025-09-30 — Earnings Commentary*

Stella Artois recorded strong performance in the United Kingdom, achieving net revenue growth of
9.0% year-over-year in 2025. Draught volume across pubs and hospitality
venues drove growth, supported by the 'Perfect Serve' chalice program and marketing campaigns highlighting Stella's
600-year brewing heritage in Leuven, Belgium. Distribution in the UK reached 89.3% ACV.
