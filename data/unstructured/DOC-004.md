# FY2024 Full-Year Results and Q4 2024 Summary

*2025-02-26 — Earnings Commentary*

Zone-by-zone results for Q4 2024:

- **North America**: revenue $3,331M (organic growth +1.7%), volume 19,516K hL, normalized EBITDA $969M (margin 29.1%).
- **Middle Americas**: revenue $4,395M (organic growth +6.6%), volume 38,907K hL, normalized EBITDA $2,227M (margin 50.7%).
- **South America**: revenue $3,473M (organic growth +3.2%), volume 44,950K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,310M (margin 37.7%).
- **EMEA**: revenue $2,424M (organic growth +8.7%), volume 24,883K hL, normalized EBITDA $776M (margin 32.0%).
- **Asia Pacific**: revenue $1,122M (organic growth -10.9%), volume 13,439K hL, normalized EBITDA $244M (margin 21.7%).

Full-year 2024: consolidated revenue $59,768M (organic growth +2.7%), volume 575,706K hL, normalized EBITDA $20,958M (margin 35.1%), net profit $5,855M. Megabrands (the company's global/multi-country brand portfolio, including Budweiser, Corona, Stella Artois and Michelob Ultra) grew revenue 4.6% for the year; Corona delivered low-teens revenue growth outside Mexico. Asia Pacific was the weakest zone for the full year, with revenue down 10.9% organically on continued China softness.
