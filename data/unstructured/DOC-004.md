# Sustainability Update: Recyclable Packaging for Vivo Splash Zero

*2025-04-22 — Sustainability*

As part of Solara FMCG Group's 2030 packaging commitments, all Vivo Splash Zero bottles
launched in Germany and the UK use 100% recycled PET (rPET). This is ahead of the
group-wide target of 70% rPET across the Non-Alcoholic portfolio by 2027.
