# Q3 2025 Earnings Commentary: Michelob ULTRA Profitability and Volume Surge

*2025-10-29 — Earnings Commentary*

Michelob ULTRA net revenue grew 13.4% year-over-year in the United States
and 13.0% in Mexico during 2025. Management noted that Michelob ULTRA's
higher net revenue per hectoliter ($75-$80/hL) and superior gross margin (15.12%+ share)
have been major contributors to EBITDA margin expansion in the North America and Middle Americas zones.
