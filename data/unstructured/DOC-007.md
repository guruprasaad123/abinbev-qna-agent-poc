# Q3 2025 Results: Zone-by-Zone Summary

*2025-10-30 — Earnings Commentary*

Zone-by-zone results for Q3 2025:

- **North America**: revenue $3,765M (organic growth -0.7%), volume 21,896K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,323M (margin 35.1%).
- **Middle Americas**: revenue $4,325M (organic growth +4.2%), volume 36,915K hL, normalized EBITDA $2,170M (margin 50.2%).
- **South America**: revenue $2,802M (organic growth +2.0%), volume 36,922K hL, normalized EBITDA $881M (margin 31.4%).
- **EMEA**: revenue $2,524M (organic growth +3.0%), volume 24,149K hL, normalized EBITDA $859M (margin 34.0%).
- **Asia Pacific**: revenue $1,533M (organic growth -9.0%), volume 22,301K hL, normalized EBITDA $452M (margin 29.5%).

Asia Pacific organic revenue declined again in Q3 2025, its steepest quarterly drop of the year, while Middle Americas remained the most consistent grower across 2025.
