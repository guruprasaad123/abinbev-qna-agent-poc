# Q2 2024 Results: Zone-by-Zone Summary

*2024-07-31 — Earnings Commentary*

Zone-by-zone results for Q2 2024:

- **North America**: revenue $3,864M (organic growth -1.3%), volume 22,639K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,338M (margin 34.6%).
- **Middle Americas**: revenue $4,522M (organic growth +5.9%), volume 38,381K hL, normalized EBITDA $2,219M (margin 49.1%).
- **South America**: revenue $2,785M (organic growth +6.1%), volume 35,969K hL, normalized EBITDA $750M (margin 26.9%).
- **EMEA**: revenue $2,301M (organic growth +10.0%), volume 23,852K hL, normalized EBITDA $721M (margin 31.3%).
- **Asia Pacific**: revenue $1,749M (organic growth -8.2%), volume 25,399K hL, normalized EBITDA $570M (margin 32.6%).

Asia Pacific organic revenue fell for a second straight quarter, driven mainly by continued softness in China, while Middle Americas and EMEA both grew normalized EBITDA faster than revenue.
