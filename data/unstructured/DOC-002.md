# Q1 2025 Earnings Commentary: Corona Cero Acceleration in Europe and Mexico

*2025-04-30 — Earnings Commentary*

Corona Cero delivered exceptional performance in early 2025, buoyed by the global Olympic campaign.
In the United Kingdom, Corona Cero net revenue expanded 22.6% year-over-year,
while Mexico posted net revenue growth of 21.9%. Distribution (ACV)
in the UK reached 79.3% as grocery and On-Premise accounts expanded shelf space.
Management highlighted that the Beyond Beer segment continues to command premium revenue per hectoliter
and higher gross margins than traditional mainstream lagers.
