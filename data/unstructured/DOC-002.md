# Q1 2025 Earnings Commentary: Beyond Beer Momentum in Europe

*2025-05-02 — Earnings Commentary*

Clearwater Zero net revenue in Germany grew 17.2% year-over-year in the
twelve months to 2025, which management attributes primarily to the March 2025
launch and continued strength in E-commerce. Distribution (ACV) in Germany
reached 76.2% by the most recent reporting month. Management noted the
Beyond Beer segment continues to grow faster than the overall portfolio.
