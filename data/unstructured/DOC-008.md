# FY2025 Full-Year Results and Q4 2025 Summary

*2026-02-11 — Earnings Commentary*

Zone-by-zone results for Q4 2025:

- **North America**: revenue $3,235M (organic growth -1.0%), volume 18,619K hL, normalized EBITDA $906M (margin 28.0%).
- **Middle Americas**: revenue $4,927M (organic growth +5.9%), volume 39,672K hL, normalized EBITDA $2,508M (margin 50.9%).
- **South America**: revenue $3,645M (organic growth +5.0%), volume 43,160K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,321M (margin 36.2%).
- **EMEA**: revenue $2,524M (organic growth +0.2%), volume 24,249K hL, normalized EBITDA $815M (margin 32.3%).
- **Asia Pacific**: revenue $1,053M (organic growth -4.3%), volume 13,334K hL, normalized EBITDA $192M (margin 18.2%).

Full-year 2025: consolidated revenue $59,320M, volume 561,100K hL, normalized EBITDA $21,223M (margin 35.8%), net profit $6,837M. Megabrands grew revenue 4.1% for the year; Corona grew revenue 8.3% outside Mexico with double-digit volume growth; Michelob Ultra became the #1 volume share gainer in the U.S. beer industry and, per the company, the leading brand by volume in that market. Asia Pacific again posted the steepest organic revenue decline of any zone for the year (-6.5%).
