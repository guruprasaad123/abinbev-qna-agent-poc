# Q2 2025 Results: Zone-by-Zone Summary

*2025-07-31 — Earnings Commentary*

Zone-by-zone results for Q2 2025:

- **North America**: revenue $3,844M (organic growth +2.2%), volume 22,376K hL, normalized EBITDA $1,372M (margin 35.7%).
- **Middle Americas**: revenue $4,340M (organic growth +5.1%), volume 38,822K hL, normalized EBITDA $2,149M (margin 49.5%).
- **South America**: revenue $2,529M (organic growth +3.6%), volume 34,199K hL, normalized EBITDA $692M (margin 27.4%).
- **EMEA**: revenue $2,489M (organic growth +5.2%), volume 24,172K hL, normalized EBITDA $800M (margin 32.1%).
- **Asia Pacific**: revenue $1,658M (organic growth -4.5%), volume 23,716K hL, normalized EBITDA $533M (margin 32.1%).

All zones except Asia Pacific grew organic revenue in Q2 2025; North America returned to positive organic growth for the first time in several quarters.
